All 55 trophies are offline single-player. No online trophies, no difficulty settings, one playthrough is enough.

⚠ One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude — one meditation in Chapter 1, on the balcony of Jason’s house, disappears after the chapter ends. Do it when Lucia offers, in the morning after Down to Earth. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story.

Roadmap to the platinum ~200 hours · PowerPyx

Stage 1 — Play the story

60–80 hours

Chapters 1–5, just enjoy it. Do the Chapter 1 meditation, start stranger chains as they unlock, scan vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals with binoculars as you go. Progress toward 100% already counts.

Yields: 13 story trophies + most side chains started

Stage 2 — 100% completion checklist

~100 hours

After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is far more forgiving than it sounds — about 60% true completion: any 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 300 stunts, all robberies, ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests.

Yields: A Long Road (gold) + most of the list

Stage 3 — Cleanup

~10 hours

Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands and whatever is left: the UFO easter egg, the unconfirmed Bad Luck, I Guess (check the sources for updates), and any stray collectibles. Then the platinum pops.

Yields: the last trophies + Vice Got a Price!

Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off for the whole run. There is free-roam after the story but no mission replay, so keep a save at the start and end of each chapter.
